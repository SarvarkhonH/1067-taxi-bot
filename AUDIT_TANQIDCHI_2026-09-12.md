### 1. Legal data residency — NOBODY MENTIONED IT. Company-ending.
Uzbekistan's personal-data law requires personal data of Uzbek citizens to be processed in a database **physically located in Uzbekistan**, registered with the state register. The entire system runs on Contabo — `1067-taxi/docs/DEPLOYMENT-GUIDE.md:79` says in plain text: `**Contabo** | ⭐ eng arzon | $6.99/oy | **Germaniya**`. In that German box sit: 750 driver phone numbers, 13 client phone numbers, GPS traces, and — worst — **scans of drivers' licences, passports and insurance documents**, written to `path.join(process.cwd(), 'uploads', 'driver-docs', String(driverId))` (`apps/api/src/modules/driver-documents/driver-documents.service.ts:34`), unencrypted, on local disk.

`grep -rin "ZRU-547|localization|shaxsiy ma'lumot|personal data|data residency"` across every `.md` in both repos returns **zero** hits about the law. Ten audits, one of them explicitly titled "safety, trust, **compliance**", scored compliance and never asked where the data physically is. The safety audit's compliance asks were "write a Privacy Policy" — writing a privacy policy for an illegally-located database is decorating the problem.

**Severity: highest.** This is not a points deduction, it is the class of thing that gets a service blocked, and it is the cheapest to have known early (it changes the hosting decision, which is currently a `[qaror]` at `BIRJOY_TAXI_MASTER.md:211`).

`grep -rin "fiscal|soliq|ofd|chek"` over `apps/api/src` + `apps/web/src`: **zero real hits** (only `timeOfDayMultiplier` substring noise). The pricing audit listed "answer the fiscal-receipt question before cutover" as a must-have — that is the only dimension that raised it, and it phrased it as a research task, not a risk. Nobody asked **what legal entity operates BirJoy taxi, and is it the same one that currently rents kas1067.** **Severity: high, and it gates launch, not follows it.**

### 5. Driver quality control does not exist for 90% of rides, and 15% of the dispatch score is therefore dead weight.
`driverRatings` has exactly one writer: `orders.service.ts:890`, reachable only through `orders.controller.ts:109 → this.orders.rateOrder(id, client.id, ...)` — an **authenticated client**. 89.4% of riders are phone callers with no account, and the client app that would authenticate the other 10% is not deployed (see #6). So `driver_ratings` will stay empty.

Meanwhile `apps/api/src/modules/dispatch/dispatch.types.ts:28` sets `rating: 0.15` and `driver-scorer.service.ts:108,135` feeds `ratingScore` into the live scorer. **15% of who gets the order is computed from a column nothing can write.** And there is no operator-side path to record "this driver was rude" against a phone caller — `support.controller.ts:13-14` gates ticket creation behind `AdminAuthGuard` with the comment "Nothing calls it today", and there is no link to it from any order page. The dispatch audit examined the scorer weights and never noticed its third-largest term is structurally constant. **Severity: high.**

### 7. i18n is BETTER than any audit said — and the one gap that matters was missed.
Nobody looked. Reality: `values/strings.xml` and `values-ru/strings.xml` both hold **237 strings** (exact parity), `LocaleManager.kt` does per-app locale properly via `AppCompatDelegate.setApplicationLocales`, and there are 257 `stringResource`/`R.string` call sites against only 26 hardcoded `Text("…")`. That is real work that ten audits scored as if it did not exist.

The actual gap: **there is no `values-uz-rCyrl`.** Uzbek Latin only. A material share of 45+ Koson drivers — the demographic that owns the ~550 cars — reads Cyrillic Uzbek far more fluently than Latin. That is a supply-acquisition barrier sitting in a `res/` folder that does not exist, and it is cheap to fix since the Latin strings are already externalised. The operator console and `apps/client` have no locale layer at all. **Severity: medium, but it is the cheapest supply lever in this whole audit.**

### 8. Zero calendar, zero weather, zero seasonality — in a town whose demand is all three.
`grep -rin "holiday|bayram|ramadan|ramazon|hayit|weather|ob-havo|qor|maktab"` over `apps/api/src`: only address strings (`6-MAKTAB MESIT`). Surge is time-of-day + zone (`pricing.service.ts:340 getTimeOfDayMultiplier`).
